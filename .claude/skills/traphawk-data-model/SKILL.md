# TrapHawk Data Model

Supabase project id: `pcjhulzyqmhrhsrgvwvx`. Query it with the Supabase `execute_sql` tool. Backend is Node/Express (`server.js`) on Railway, frontend is Vite/React in root `src/` (NOT `vite-project/`).

## The pipeline in one paragraph

Math picks the side, Claude narrates. `api/cron/pre-analyze-games.js` computes per-side edges (edge calculator for team sports, dedicated models in `docs/models/` for tennis, UFC, soccer), then calls Claude only to write the analysis (Sonnet 5.5 for Play and above and Trap tiles, Haiku 5.5 for Lean, Leg and Skip tiles since 2026-10-13, game_analysis.model_used says which). The LLM never chooses the side. Shadow sports (`SHADOW_SPORTS`: the soccer family plus NCAAF, the fail soft mirror, the live truth is the publish flags on sport_dials) store edges and analyses but never publish picks. `SOCCER_1X2_SPORTS` is a separate set that answers which model prices a game, `SHADOW_SPORTS` answers whether it publishes. Tennis left the shadow list 2026-08-10 and publishes through the ladder (multiplier 0.50 at first, 1 since 2026-09-21), UFC left it 2026-08-25 on 81 graded shadow reads at 48-33 with measured k 4.43 (UFC picks are real but rare under the 2pp gate), and NFL went LIVE 2026-09-09 ahead of the opener with moneyline and spread publishing at multipliers 0.25 and 0.10 (both 1 since 2026-09-21) and totals muted by publish_total 0. None of those three is a shadow sport any more and none is judged against the promotion bar. NCAAF was planned to flip at the 2026-08-29 opener, the flip was not taken, and it then went live per market through promote_ready_markets on the shadow bar: totals 2026-09-27, moneyline 2026-10-11 (model_weight_changes 37 and 46). NCAAF spread is still shadow. Shadow promotion for player sports is judged by `shadow_model_readiness()`: 75 graded publishable reads, actual at or above fair implied, AND positive publishable units. Never a raw read count.

## Narration checker (shadow, 2026-10-15)

`narration_checks` holds one row per fresh game narration from `api/cron/pre-analyze-games.js`: the Haiku 5.5 (MODELS.UTILITY) verdict on the draft against the matchup data it was written from (`lib/services/narration-checker.js`, build_queue 90). Columns: game_key, sport, game_date, tier, narration_model, checker_model, verdict (pass, fail or error), issues (jsonb list of claim and problem), error, analysis_snippet, input_tokens, output_tokens, cost_usd, blocked (always false in shadow), checked_at. Shadow for two weeks: nothing is blocked and nothing on the pricing or publish path reads it. The run's tally rides in cron_job_logs details.checker (calls, pass, fail, error, input, output, cost). Judge it by fail rate per narration_model and by whether the fails name real errors, then the owner decides whether a failing draft is rewritten and fact-check-picks is unscheduled.

## NFL player props (shadow, 2026-09-11)

`prop_reads` is the shadow record for NFL player props, one row per (event, market, player), written by `api/cron/analyze-nfl-props.js` twice daily and graded from the ESPN final box score minutes after the game ends (`?mode=boxscore`, run by every capture-prop-closes tick, by its own cron grade-nfl-props-boxscore on the other five minute marks from 12:00 to 03:59 UTC since 2026-10-18, and before the daily grade, cron_job_logs grade-nfl-props-boxscore, since 2026-10-16 at owner direction), with `?mode=grade` settling anything left against `nfl_player_game_stats` (nflverse, synced Tuesdays) and logging every box score grade the stat file disagrees with as box_mismatches (never re-flipped, a regrade goes through allow_regrade). The formula (`lib/services/prop-edge.js`) has the same shape as every team market: anchor at the devigged consensus over price at the median book line (`player_props`, 8 books), model from the player's own game log, damp the disagreement by `prop_claim_damp` times games over `prop_min_games`, band the pp. Every weight is an `NFL_props` row in `sport_dials`. Five over/under markets are modeled from the game log (pass yds, pass TDs, rush yds, rec yds, receptions). Since 2026-10-12 anytime TD (`player_anytime_td`) is modeled too, stored as line 0.5 with over meaning yes, from red zone usage instead of the game log: `nfl_player_redzone` (one row per game_id and player_id from nflverse play by play, synced daily by `/cron/sync-nflverse-redzone`) carries rz_carries, gl_carries (5 or closer), rz_targets, ez_targets (thrown to the goal line or past it), rz_rush_tds, rz_rec_tds and the team totals team_rz_carries and team_rz_targets. TD_RATES in prop-edge.js turns each opportunity into expected touchdowns, P(yes) is one minus e to the minus that sum, and the anchor is the median yes price with the vig left in (books post no no price). The same read fills the v1 and v2 columns, `model_mean` and `v2_mean` hold the expected touchdowns, `model_sigma` is null, the under side is a no that most books do not offer, so judge anytime TD on its over rows. It grades from rushing_tds plus receiving_tds. Players with no touches have no nflverse stat line and void rather than lose. Nothing publishes: no ai_suggestions row, no digest tile, no alert, so `prop_reads` never touches mv_public_record. Judge it like any shadow sport: graded reads at 2pp+ against the anchor, won minus anchor implied, by market and by band. Since 2026-09-14 every row also carries a v2 shadow read (`v2_mean`, `v2_prob`, `v2_edge_pp`, `v2_side`, `v2_tier`, `v2_outcome`, `v2_factors`): the v1 baseline scaled by the opponent's allowance per game against league average (`prop_opp_weight`), the team's implied points from the median spread and total against the slate average (`prop_env_weight`), and wind on the passing markets outdoors (`prop_wind_weight`), skipped when Sleeper or the official report lists the player out (`v2_factors.skipped`). The grader settles `v2_outcome` from the same stat line. `actual_outcome` = `void` means the player had no stat line after the week's file landed (a book void), or since 2026-10-18 that the game went final with no box score line while ESPN's summary listed the player Out (the box score grader voids it at the final); exclude void from every record. The first graded week (2026-09-14, v1 only) went 192-170 with a flat ladder and negative price CLV, so read the v2 columns as the candidate and the v1 columns as the frozen regime. Since 2026-10-12 (build_queue 94) `sync-nfl-props?mode=close` (cron capture-prop-closes, every 15 minutes from 12:00 to 03:59 UTC) fetches NFL events starting inside the next 25 minutes and writes the closing consensus onto prop_reads: close_line, close_over_prob (devigged, same method as anchor_prob), close_over_price, close_under_price, close_books, close_captured_at, and CLV per side, clv_pp and beat_close for the v1 side, v2_clv_pp and v2_beat_close for the v2 side. At the same line clv_pp is devigged pp gained (positive beat the close). When the line moved clv_pp is null and beat_close follows the move (an over beat it when the line rose). The view `prop_clv_scorecard` rolls v2 reads at 2pp or more up by market and band. Reads priced before 2026-10-12 have no close. Since 2026-10-12 the shown reads (v2 at 4pp or more, upcoming) also carry `narration`, two or three plain sentences from Sonnet (Sonnet 5.5 since 2026-10-13) written by `api/cron/narrate-nfl-props.js` after every read run, plus `narration_detail` (the two paragraph Read more write up from the same call, since 2026-10-12), with `narration_side`, `narration_line`, `narration_model` and `narrated_at`. The consensus and close prices (consensus_over_price, consensus_under_price, close_over_price, close_under_price) average across books as implied probability since 2026-10-13 (PR 231). Rows before that averaged American odds, so an absolute price under 100 on an older row is an artifact, the anchor probabilities and edges were never affected. `/api/props` shows the narration only while narration_side and narration_line still match the read, so a flipped side or a moved line never shows stale text, and the next run narrates it again.

## Tier system (CALIBRATED edge in percentage points vs implied probability)

| Tier | Rule |
|---|---|
| Sharp Take | published edge_pp >= 10pp. The tier is nothing but the pp band since 2026-09-10 (directive 16): price rails and the exposure guard deduct from the claim BEFORE the ladder (lib/services/price-penalties.js: -150 or heavier minus chalk_penalty_pp, +300 or longer minus longshot_penalty_pp scaled by price, floored at 2pp; exposure-guard.js: minus exposure_guard_pp after two straight graded team losses), and edge_pp stores the adjusted claim with a "Chalk price:", "Longshot price:", "No starters:" (MLB read without the Probable starters factor, no_starter_penalty_pp, since 2026-10-03) or "Exposure guard:" marker in reasoning. Rows stamped before 2026-09-10 carry the old label fence (no Sharp Take at -150 or heavier) and ceiling (nothing above Lean at +300 or longer) with an unpenalized edge_pp |
| Strong Play | edge 7-10pp. Merged into Play 2026-08-10 and RESTORED 2026-08-16 by owner decision (7pp is the betting floor, that band earns its own label); only rows stamped 08-10 to 08-16 carry Play for a 7-10pp edge |
| Play | edge 4-7pp |
| Lean | edge >= 2pp (2pp is the floor for a published pick) |
| Skip | edge between -2pp and 2pp |
| Trap | lure-based, side priced <= -2pp that casual bettors are drawn to (`lib/services/trap-detector.js`). NOT simply the inverse of a pick |
| Leg | model probability at or above the `leg_prob_floor` dial (since 2026-09-21: `__all__` 0.75, NFL 0.80, MLB 0.70; owner: near sure things only, the flat 0.65 let every NFL -200 favorite in) but no 2pp edge (payout too thin). Tracked for parlay building |

Edges are scaled by `edge_calibration` multipliers (keys `<Sport>:<market>`, `<Sport>`, `__global__`) before tiering, and since 2026-09-21 (directive 25 as amended, owner: no haircut out of fear) EVERY multiplier is 1: they are dials, no weekly refit (edge_calibration_weekly and both band refit jobs are unscheduled), and one leaves 1 only through the three test sweep or an owner call with a model_weight_changes row. The publish gate binds on the raw claim ALONE (2pp), the multiplier sizes only the label, and a scaled claim under 2pp publishes at the Lean floor. A market mute is `publish_<market>` 0 on the dial board (MLB and NFL totals since 2026-09-21), never a zero multiplier, and `promote_ready_markets()` lifts a mute by flipping that dial with the multiplier at 1. The band maps (`edge_band_calibration`, `edge_band_calibration_raw`) are identity and lib/services/band-calibration.js is identity in code, so the tier is the raw claim times the multiplier minus the price rails and nothing else.  The bands are buckets with floors in `bucket_targets` (pp delivered over break even at the price: Lean 2, Play 4, Strong Play 7, Sharp Take 10, sport override allowed); `bucket_scorecard(p_days, p_min_n, p_regime_floor)` scores every sport and `__all__` per bucket on the public record's population, honoring `band_fit_floors`, and `file_bucket_scorecard()` (cron bucket-scorecard-weekly, Mondays 06:50 UTC) files it to agent_reports as agent bucket-scorecard with the next sweep target. `replay_buckets()` gives the same table per replay variant and `file_replay_candidates` prints it on every candidate. Live multipliers are read from the table, never from this file: `select key, multiplier, measured_k, source, updated_at from edge_calibration order by key;` (the ops check found this paragraph stale on three keys on 2026-09-10, so numbers no longer live here). Decisions by date, for context only: MLB ml floored at 0.25 on 2026-08-24, MLB spreads re-entered 2026-08-10, MLB totals re-entered on probation 2026-08-31 under the band aware map (PR 110), Tennis:ml refit weekly since 2026-09-07, UFC:ml promoted out of shadow 2026-08-25 (claims average 0.35pp, so UFC is mostly a board, leg, and trap product, not a pick engine), NFL seeded 2026-09-09 at go-live with totals muted. Legs are GIMMES only: calibrated model probability at or above the leg_prob_floor dial (sport_dials __all__ 0.75, NFL 0.80, MLB 0.70, UFC 1.01 which keeps UFC out of the pool, code fallback 0.65 in lib/services/edge-calculator.js 160), no loosening (a market-anchored second path shipped and was reverted the same day, 2026-08-25, at owner direction). The quiet-day Discord content comes from the morning board's presentation-only high-percenter list (fairImplied 60%+ favorites the model agrees with, from game_analysis, never published or graded). Discord (owner redesign 2026-09-21) is three channels, every post an embed with no site link: board (DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL: the 08:00 MT morning board and Strong Play or Sharp Take entry alerts), receipts (DISCORD_WEBHOOK_RECEIPTS_URL: 07:30 MT card of yesterday's settled picks by tier plus the 3, 7 and 30 day record, every number from mv_public_record), and model (DISCORD_WEBHOOK_MODEL_URL: an hourly feed of new model_weight_changes rows and new or amended directives, cursor in `discord_feed_cursor`, plus the Monday 10:00 MT dial board embed, one field per sport with multipliers, the week's moves and the bucket floors). A channel without its own webhook falls back to the board one, and that is the live setup by owner decision (2026-10-01): all three posts land in one Discord channel through DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL until a TrapHawk server with its own channels exists, the other two variables are unset on purpose until then, and fallback true in the log is not a problem. Posters: api/cron/discord-morning-board.js, discord-receipts.js, discord-model-feed.js; cron_job_logs rows discord-morning-board, discord-receipts, discord-model-feed, discord-dial-board. A `reprice-pending-picks` cron demotes pending Play moneyline picks to Lean when the market fades their side by 1pp of implied probability inside 90 minutes of first pitch.

## Record domains in ai_suggestions

Rows are namespaced by `session_id` prefix, one row per game per domain per day:

- `auto_digest_YYYY-MM-DD` = published picks
- `auto_digest_trap_YYYY-MM-DD` = trap calls. actual_outcome is the TRAPPED side's own result: a won trap row means the bait side won (verified 2026-10-12, 116 of 116 moneyline trap rows match the final score; this line said the opposite before and the landing page, the digest band and Discord receipts read it backwards until build_queue 95). So the Trap row of mv_public_record is the bait's record and its roi_units is what betting the bait cost. A trap says do not bet it (owner 2026-10-12), so the headline stat is that cost: negative units mean the warning worked
- `auto_digest_leg_YYYY-MM-DD` = legs (sides at or above leg_prob_floor), outcomes read straight
- `auto_digest_alt_spread_YYYY-MM-DD` and `auto_digest_alt_total_YYYY-MM-DD` = the spotlight alternates (lib/services/alt-markets.js): a second graded market on a game whose headline pick is another market. They ARE in mv_public_record (session_id like auto_digest%) and compete with the headline row for the (home, away, game_date, bet_type) slot, the later revision winning. Directive 17's raw claim check covers them since 2026-10-03.

A partial unique index (`uq_ai_suggestions_auto_digest_game`) dedupes on (session_id, home, away, game_date), and since 2026-08-12 the upsert matches on `odds_event_id` per domain FIRST, so a start-time re-emit revises the existing row instead of duplicating it (three tennis duplicates double counted the record before this). Rows with `voided_at` set are retroactively voided (published under a later-discovered defect, or duplicates) and excluded from mv_public_record; filter `voided_at is null` in any raw query that should match the public record. Key columns: sport, home_team, away_team, game_date, bet_type, pick, odds, edge_pp, tier, actual_outcome, reasoning, `odds_event_id` (the Odds API event the pick was priced from, on every row since 2026-08-09), and for traps lure_score plus trap_signals, for legs model_prob plus implied_prob.

THE FROZEN COHORT: 529 rows share `resolved_at = '2026-08-09 12:13:08.323644-06'`. They were regraded, then restored at owner direction the same day after six forensic audits returned a mixed verdict. NEVER re-flip them in any sweep. Details and row lists live in agent_reports (2026-08-09).

## Settlement and the one clock

Same-game identity resolves in strict order: `odds_event_id` (primary-key hit against `odds_api_scores`), then kickoff instant within 3 hours, then exact Denver calendar day. America/Denver is the ONLY timezone that may produce a calendar day anywhere in the pipeline, via `shared/site-day.js` (lib/services/sport-day.js delegates to it). A tripwire test fails the build on any new UTC day derivation. `game_results.date` rows stamped before 2026-07-25 were UTC-derived and can be one day late for evening games; do not treat them as ground truth for which night a game was played.

## mv_public_record is the ONLY source for public percentages

Every number shown to users (hero record, tier records, trap record, leg pool) comes from `mv_public_record`. Do not compute win rates from raw ai_suggestions and do not use `mv_model_accuracy` (full-history, reserved for calibration). The two will disagree and the MV is right: it starts at the graded era (May 10 2026), drops soccer v1, and dedupes per domain (picks, traps, legs separately).

Shape: one row per (period_bucket, dimension_type, dimension_value). period_bucket in `all | last_30d | last_7d | last_3d`. dimension_type in `overall | sport | bet_type | tier`. Count columns: won, lost, push, pending, total, plus settled_with_odds, avg_decimal_odds, roi_units.

```sql
select dimension_value, won, lost, push, pending
from mv_public_record
where period_bucket = 'last_30d' and dimension_type = 'tier';
```

CRITICAL gotcha: recreating the MV drops its grants. After any DROP plus CREATE, re-apply:

```sql
GRANT SELECT ON public.mv_public_record TO anon, authenticated, service_role;
```

Skipping this silently zeroes every public stat on the site.

- `pick_clv` (view): closing line value per moneyline pick, joined by event id or kickoff instant. `clv_pp` positive means the pick beat the close. The earliest honest signal of edge drift.
- `odds_api_scores`: finals from The Odds API keyed by `event_id` with real commence instants. The settlement grader's first stop and the independent cross-check source against ESPN-backed `game_results`.
- `player_props`: raw props market rows (event, market, player_key, line, over/under/yes prices, per book), NFL preseason collection first. `player_game_stats`: ESPN box-score stats, football-shaped columns only.
- `nfl_injury_reports` (since 2026-09-14): the official NFL practice and game status report per player per week from the nflverse injuries release, synced every 6 hours by `sync-nfl-injuries`. `report_status` is the Friday designation (Out, Doubtful, Questionable, null for none), `practice_status` the last participation (Full, Limited, Did Not Participate), keyed (season, week, team, player_key) with team as the nflverse club code (`teamAbbr` in lib/services/nfl-inactives.js maps full names). It is narration context for NFL (pre-analyze reads it before news_cache), and since 2026-10-13 the narration also gets the priced status list from edge_factors.injuryReport as the newer word (pricedInjuryText, build_queue 106) and the replay input for a practice participation factor after the freeze (build_queue 35). The injury factor's MATH still reads the ESPN status list. `nfl_depth_charts`: since 2026-09-15 a rolling 28 day window of each club's depth chart snapshots from the same release (unique on team, snapshot_at and slot, only snapshots newer than stored are written each run), `pos_rank` 1 is the starter for a slot; `nfl_depth_rank_floor` (view) is each player's best rank over the window, read by the injury factor's depth gate in lib/services/football-injuries.js (the position cost times a per position ladder share for the rank: only the starting quarterback, WR1 through WR4 tapering, one starter per line slot, half for the second back, corner or lineman; unlisted times `injury_depth_unknown_weight`), and the report lines in `edge_factors.injuryReport` carry `depth_rank` and `depth_weight` so a read shows what each absence cost. `game_analysis.edge_factors.injuryReport` ({home, away} lists of {player, position, status}) is the report an NFL read was priced on; `watch-nfl-inactives` (every 15 minutes, 3 hours before kickoff) diffs ESPN's game summary against it, and a player newly Out marks the analysis stale and re-runs pre-analyze-NFL, so a late scratch revises the pick through the same formula and leaves a tier_history entry. Its cron_job_logs details carry the scratches it found.
- `admin_users` (since 2026-09-15): the admin allowlist, one row per email, seeded with every account at the time (owner: "add all users so far to admin ability"). `requireAdmin` in api/admin-dashboard.js passes an email on the ADMIN_EMAILS env list (the floor, so the owner can never be locked out) or on this table, cached five minutes. `GET /api/admin/dials?sport=X&days=N` (api/admin-dials.js, page /admin/dials) is the read only dial board per market: the merged `sport_dials` board with the scope that won, the rails, the publication flags, the `edge_calibration` multipliers, the raw band map, the last ten `model_weight_changes`, and every `game_analysis` read in the window with its factor stack, attached picks and `shadow_reads_graded` rows (NFL_props returns `prop_reads` with the v1 and v2 columns). It writes nothing: a dial moves only through sport_dials plus model_weight_changes under directives 7, 8 and 21.
- `lookback_reads` (since 2026-09-15, build_queue 40): one row per settled anchored football read, written by `run_football_lookback(p_days)` (cron football-lookback-weekly, Tuesdays 09:00 MT): `home_won`, `anchor_toward_winner`, `net_toward_winner`, `market_move_home_pp` (the h2h close against the read price, positive toward home), `close_toward_winner`, and `factors` [{family, factor, impact_pp, toward_winner, toward_close, decisive}] where decisive means removing that factor alone would have flipped the side. The function files an agent_reports row from agent football-lookback with the per sport headline, the per factor family table and the misses. `band_fit_floors`: per sport regime line honored by `refresh_edge_band_calibration_raw` (NFL and NCAAF 2026-09-16 after the depth gate and seed_weight changes, the twelve soccer competitions 2026-10-14 for the soccer 1X2 v2 setup); rows before a sport's floor never enter its band fit or the `__all__` pool.
- `tracked_bets` (since 2026-10-16, PR 254): one row per signed in user per bet they tapped Track bet on (user_id, item_key, kind game or prop, item jsonb snapshot, game_time, created_at; primary key user_id plus item_key). Written straight from the browser through the anon client under four own-row RLS policies, never by a cron. item_key is `game:<game_key>:<pick text>` or `prop:<player_key>|<market>|<side>|<line>|<commence_time>`. A visitor keeps the same list in local storage (`traphawk.tracked.v1`) and it moves into the table on sign in. Grades are never stored here: `POST /api/tracked/outcomes` reads them from ai_suggestions and prop_reads by key.
- `edge_calibration` plus `market_shadow_calibration(since date)`: multipliers and the shadow grader that lets muted markets earn their way back (re-enable bar 52.4 percent at -110 on a real sample).

## Player sports are different on purpose

Tennis record columns store 30-DAY form, not season records. UFC records come from `ufc_fighters`. Never compare these to season standings (this caused false record-mismatch alarms). The integrity sweep's records verifier already excludes Tennis, UFC, and Golf. ESPN's UFC feed covers UFC events only, so PFL/ACA cards on the MMA odds feed have no fighter data.

## Cron architecture

pg_cron jobs fire `net.http_post` at Railway `/cron/*` endpoints authenticated by a secret in the URL. Extract it in SQL when triggering jobs manually:

```sql
SELECT net.http_post(
  url := 'https://craycrayparlayapp-production.up.railway.app/cron/<endpoint>?secret=' ||
    (SELECT substring(command FROM 'secret=([^&]+)') FROM cron.job WHERE jobname = 'pre-analyze-mlb' LIMIT 1),
  headers := jsonb_build_object('Content-Type','application/json'),
  body := '{}'::jsonb, timeout_milliseconds := 300000);
```

Job schedules live in `cron.job`. Some Claude-calling jobs (fact-check-picks) do NOT write cron_job_logs, so absence of logs is not absence of runs. The RSS news path is gone since 2026-09-14 (owner, build_queue 36): `news_articles` and `news_sources` are frozen history, nothing writes them, and the narration prompt's "Recent news" lines, the digest fact sheet articles, the fact check and (until 2026-10-12) the golf notes come from ESPN's news API through `lib/services/espn-news.js` (one cached call per sport per 30 minutes, articles tagged to teams and athletes, no model call). `nfl_player_status` is the daily Sleeper snapshot (`sync-sleeper-players`, 06:30 MT): injury status and notes, practice participation, depth chart position and order per rostered player, a replay input next to `nfl_injury_reports`.

## Writing style is enforced in code

`WRITING_STYLE` in `lib/services/claude.js` bans em dashes, en dashes, semicolons, and arrows in all model output. `complete()` injects it automatically. Any new raw `messages.create` call site must import and include it, and your own prose to the user follows the same rule.


## Publication per market (2026-09-13)

Whether a (sport, market) reaches the graded record is three dials on `sport_dials`: `publish_ml`, `publish_spread`, `publish_total`, 1 or 0 per sport (`__all__` 1; the shadow sports NCAAF, EPL, MLS, Soccer, World Cup, Champions League, Europa League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Copa America, Euros start at 0 on all three). The SHADOW_SPORTS constants in code are only the fail-soft mirror. `/api/digest` sends `publishMarkets` and the board withholds a shadow market's read (src/lib/publish-flags.js). A mute is the same dial at 0 for a sport that otherwise publishes (MLB and NFL totals since 2026-09-21); the zero multiplier mute is gone. `shadow_reads_graded` and `shadow_market_readiness()` are the shadow ledger (soccer games join game_results on `soccer_team_key()` too, a normalized club key plus the `soccer_team_aliases` table, because Odds API and ESPN spell clubs differently; add an alias row when a soccer game goes ungraded); the soccer goal model keeps `soccer_match_history` (ESPN finals for the eight soccer competitions back to August 2024, kept apart from game_results so a deep history walk never grades old rows), `soccer_team_strengths` (current attack and defense per pool and club, team_key is the JS soccerTeamKey) and `soccer_model_fits` (per fit: competition rates, rho, walk forward replay vs the close in evaluation), written daily by /cron/fit-soccer-strengths, with the `goal_model_weight` dial (0) setting its blend weight; `historical_closing_lines` holds closes backfilled from the Odds API historical snapshots (last quote before kickoff, same shape as closing_lines, never read by CLV on real picks), worked from the `historical_odds_jobs` queue (one row per sport, month window and market list, status, credits_used) by /cron/backfill-historical-odds every 15 minutes; a window whose markets are all player props fills `historical_prop_closes` instead (last quote before kickoff per game, market, player and book, Over and Under on one row, nflverse season and week), US team sport windows (NFL, NCAAF, MLB, NBA, NHL on h2h, spreads, totals back to the 2023 seasons) also write the ESPN finals of their days to `historical_game_results` (kept apart from game_results so no old final meets a pending pick), and `game_results_with_history` (game_results plus those finals) is what the replay's history mode reads for scores and as-of team records; the `historical_prop_results` view grades the consensus close (the line most books hang, mean devigged over probability) against `nfl_player_game_stats` (result over, under, push, or null while the stat line is missing); `promote_ready_markets()` (daily) opens a market that clears the bar; `file_replay_candidates()` (Sunday sweep) files dial candidates in build_queue. `home_margin_shift` is the MLB spread transform dial.
