## Synthesis per candidate

### Trapline — the analytical insider tool
- **Native surfaces:** Hero/sub, App Store, About, CTA (the name verbs cleanly as object: "see today's trapline"), email signoff
- **Forced surfaces:** IG bio reads a touch clinical
- **Why it works:** The name does the work. It's a noun, a verb-by-implication, and it encodes the wedge directly. Every analytical or product-utility surface feels native; every casual-personality surface is slightly cooler than it needs to be.
- **Trade-off:** Best across the surfaces that matter for *trust and conversion*. Loses to DegenMath on *personality and shareability*.

### DegenMath — the dual-register pratfall
- **Native surfaces:** X/Twitter bio, Instagram bio, tagline, email signoff — anywhere personality is the point
- **Forced surfaces:** App Store (review risk + name reads off-brand to Apple's conservative reviewers), About page (meta-recursion: "DegenMath was built by a degen…"), secondary CTA (the name can't easily appear in a CTA without sounding stuck-up)
- **Why it shines:** *"Built by degens. Run by math."* is genuinely best-in-class. The name names the contradiction. Social bios write themselves.
- **Why it risks:** Apple App Store review + Stripe content-policy + investor positioning are all softer-yes when the brand has "Degen" in it. Real (not catastrophic) friction in surfaces where it counts.

### Fadeline — the safe middle
- **Native surfaces:** Every surface (the name is a clean verb-noun)
- **Forced surfaces:** None outright — but also none dazzle
- **Why it works:** Reads native everywhere. Easiest sweep across all 8.
- **Trade-off:** Lacks the brand-spike Trapline gets at wedge moments (App Store, About) AND the personality spike DegenMath gets at social. Strong third on every individual surface; not the winner on any.

## Recommendation

**Ship with Trapline.**

The wedge-encoding moments are where the name has to land hardest — hero, App Store, About, CTA. Trapline is the strongest on every one of those. DegenMath's high points (taglines, social bios) are genuinely better than Trapline's, but its low points (App Store review risk, About-page recursion, business-context positioning) introduce real ongoing friction.

**The decoupling move:** Use Trapline as the brand name everywhere. Put degen voice in the *copy* and in the *De-Genny chatbot* — not in the name. You get the analytical-trust upside of Trapline AND the degen-personality energy of DegenMath, by separating the two. The Landing's just-shipped trading-terminal aesthetic was already this strategy in visual form. Trapline extends it to the wordmark.

**Hold Fadeline as the fallback** if WHOIS or trademark checks knock Trapline out.

**Before any domain purchase:** WHOIS + TESS trademark scan + social handle availability on the recommended name and a runner-up. Apple App Store review risk on DegenMath specifically should be quick-checked against Apple's content guidelines if it stays in play.
