## My honest read

Two genuine finalists: **Trapline** and **DegenMath.** They tie at 97. They're meaningfully different bets:

- **Trapline** is the "let the math speak" play. Reads as an analytical insider tool to both ICPs. Lower-risk. Easier to defend to investors / payment processors / app stores. Will land harder with sharp-curious.
- **DegenMath** is the "name the contradiction" play. Builds the dual-audience tension into the brand itself. Higher-ceiling — most defensible against future competitors copying us, because the name IS the positioning. But it carries real risk on payment processors (Stripe sometimes flags "degen*") and Apple App Store review.

If you forced me to pick one, I'd pick **Trapline** for the launch — it's the safer ship without sacrificing brand-fit. **DegenMath** could absolutely work but I'd want to verify the processor / app store risk before committing money to the .com.

**Fadeline** is the strong third — same upside as Trapline but the wedge encoding is less explicit. Worth holding as a backup if Trapline domains turn out to be unavailable or trademark-collided.

## Before any domain purchase

Required for whichever candidate(s) Vince advances:

1. **WHOIS lookup** at Namecheap / GoDaddy on `.com` and `.app`. Confirm not just "no active brand" but actual availability and price band.
2. **TESS trademark search** (USPTO) on the exact name + key variants in sports betting / sports media / gambling classes.
3. **Apple App Store + Stripe content-policy check** specifically for "Degen*" if DegenMath is in play.
4. **Social handle check** on X/Twitter, Instagram, TikTok — name needs to be available on at least the first two.
5. **Pronunciation test** with 3-5 people not in the industry — confirm first-try pronounce + spell.
