## The actual decision blocking final lock

**Domain strategy is the only real choice.** Three options:

### Option A — Premium spend on `fadeline.com` ($3k–$10k+)
- Buy from Atom marketplace
- Cleanest brand position; `.com` is the default trust signal
- Real cash outlay for a pre-revenue launch

### Option B — `.app` as primary, `.com` later
- Buy `fadeline.app` cheap (likely $20/yr at Namecheap if confirmed available)
- Modern SaaS lane uses `.app` (linear.app, lovable.app, vercel.app, etc.) — not a stigma
- Hedge: snag `getfadeline.com` ($12/yr) as a redirect for users who type `.com` reflexively
- Risk: if Cray ever gets big, the `.com` price will go UP, not down. May regret not buying now.

### Option C — Best-of-both wait + ship
- Ship the soft launch on `fadeline.app` + `getfadeline.com` (combined ~$30/yr)
- Watch `fadeline.com` for the August 2026 expiration — if not renewed, it might drop back into general availability via DropCatch
- If Atom price is reasonable ($3-5k) at any point, buy then

**My recommendation:** **Option C** for this week (soft-launch posture, lean), pivot to A or hold at C based on (a) Atom's actual asking price and (b) early traction signals after soft launch.
