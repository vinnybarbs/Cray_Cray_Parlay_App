## Why AI-SEO is now a first-class naming dimension

Traditional SEO predicts whether Google ranks your name. AI-SEO predicts whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews, and Claude **cite** a sports-betting product by that name. The difference matters because:

- ~45% of Google searches now show AI Overviews
- AI Overviews reduce clicks to websites by up to 58%
- Brands are 6.5× more likely to be cited via third-party sources than their own domains
- "Best [category]" queries are increasingly answered by AI before a user clicks anything

A name with strong AI-search-citation collision (e.g., shares the name with an Oprah's Book Club bestseller) will be effectively invisible in AI-mediated product discovery — even if traditional SEO is winnable.

## Why three of four candidates fell on AI-SEO

The pattern reveals something about modern naming: **competitive brand surveillance now extends past traditional SERPs into the AI-tools ecosystem itself**. Both Surfaced and Gauge are AEO-category products — they exist *because of* the rise of AI search. We weren't aware they existed in Phase 3 because they're new and don't yet dominate traditional SEO — but they ARE positioned to dominate AI citations in any "AI visibility" or "brand monitoring" query, which secondarily contaminates their brand names for AI-search purposes.

TheTell fell for a different reason: cultural footprint. Oprah's Book Club selections get massive third-party citation density (book reviews, interviews, recommendations, Wikipedia, Goodreads) — and AI search heavily favors third-party-cited sources. A 2025 NYT bestseller is essentially uncatchable.
