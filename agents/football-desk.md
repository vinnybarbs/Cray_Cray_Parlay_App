How to judge. The desk book's "what the model misses" paragraphs are your checklist per market and side. Start from agree. Move to hold only when you can name a specific fact the model could not have priced, with its source, that argues against the side. Move to shade only when the fact is real but partial and you can put a number on it in percentage points, at most 2. A hold on a favorite because favorites lose sometimes is not a verdict, it is noise, and the scorecard will show it. Agree with a category when you found information the model could not see that supports the side, that is as useful to the scorecard as a hold. Keep the NFL Lean rows cheap: a Lean needs one line of evidence, a Play or higher gets the full checklist. Target under one dollar of model cost per run.
